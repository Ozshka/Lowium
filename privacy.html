Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lowium ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website located at https://ozshka.github.io/Lowium/ (the "Service").

1. Who We Are

Lowium is a personal blogging platform. The data controller responsible for your personal data is the owner of this project. You can contact us at: support.lowium@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

When you register and use Lowium, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and password. Passwords are securely hashed and never stored in plain text. Your email is used only for authentication and is not shared with other users.
  • Profile Information: Your display name, unique tag (username), bio, region, and avatar image (if you choose to upload one). This information is publicly visible on the Service.
  • Content: Posts, comments, and images you upload or publish on the Service. This content is publicly visible.
  • Verification Data: If you request verification to publish content, you may submit a short message explaining your request. This message is visible only to the site administrator.
  • Technical Data: We may indirectly collect basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type through our infrastructure providers (Supabase and GitHub Pages) for security and operational purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To allow you to publish posts, comment, and interact with other users (after verification).
  • To display your public profile information to other users.
  • To review and process verification requests.
  • To ensure the security and proper functioning of the Service.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on your consent (which you provide when registering) and for the performance of a contract (providing you with access to the Service).

5. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. However, we use third-party service providers to operate the Service:

  • Supabase: Used for authentication (storing email/password) and database storage (posts, profiles, comments, verification requests). Supabase stores data on servers located outside of Russia (EU/US). By using the Service, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed in these jurisdictions.
  • GitHub Pages: Used to host the static frontend files. No personal data is stored on GitHub Pages itself.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our active databases. Note that some content (such as posts or comments) may remain if you choose to delete your account but not your content.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information through your profile settings.
  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (by deleting your account).

To exercise these rights, contact us at support.lowium@gmail.com.

8. Verification and Content Creation

Lowium uses a manual verification system. Newly registered users can read and like content but cannot publish posts or comments until an administrator approves their verification request. The verification message you submit is stored securely and is only accessible to the administrator.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Passwords are stored using strong hashing algorithms. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support.lowium@gmail.com.
